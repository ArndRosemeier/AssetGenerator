MakeHuman / MPFB humans (Character Studio export)

Bodies, face morphs and civilian clothes come from MakeHuman / MPFB and
community clothes fetched into tools/character_studio/makehuman_extra_assets/.
The export writes engine-agnostic .glb files here. Character Studio is one
consumer; other projects should copy or load from this folder.

Rebuild:

    python tools/sync_character_studio_assets.py
